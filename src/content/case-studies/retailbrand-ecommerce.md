## Challenge
RetailBrand was struggling to scale their paid advertising campaigns while maintaining profitability. Their cost per acquisition was too high, and they couldn't effectively target high-intent shoppers.

## Solution
We redesigned their PPC strategy with a focus on product-specific campaigns, shopping feed optimization, and advanced audience targeting. We implemented automated bidding strategies and continuous A/B testing.

## Results
RetailBrand achieved a 300% return on ad spend (ROAS) while reducing their cost per acquisition by 40%. Their e-commerce revenue grew by 200% in the first quarter of our partnership.
